About

Making complexity
approachable.

I'm a library and instructional technology professional with six-plus years of experience at the intersection of technical support, instructional design, and people-centered service. Currently, I serve as Lead Librarian & Instructional Design Technician at South College in Orlando, where I manage helpdesk operations, build Canvas course pages, and keep an entire campus ecosystem of learners connected to the resources they need.

Outside the library, I'm a graduate student at the University of Central Florida pursuing an M.A. in Literary Studies/History and a Graduate Certificate in Digital Humanities in the Age of AI — because I believe the most interesting questions live at the border of the humanistic and the technological.

I also write. For community organizations, wellness platforms, and my own desk drawer. Words have always been the tool I trust most.

Writing & Publications

Words that do work.

I write for libraries, wellness communities, and anyone willing to sit with a hard question. These pieces represent my range — from environmental advocacy to relational psychology to personal essay.

Wellness · &Rise 2025

Gut Feelings You Should Never Ignore in Relationships

Your nervous system is wired to pick up on subtle cues long before your conscious mind processes them. This piece explores the science behind intuition, somatic psychology, and how to honor inner signals to build healthier connections — written for the &Rise community of women survivors.

"What feels like a gut feeling is often your body noticing patterns of behavior, tone, or energy shifts. Ignoring these signals can lead to repeated hurt, while honoring them can help you establish boundaries, protect your peace, and build healthier connections."

Personal Essay · &Rise May 2025

The Younger Me Is Watching, And She Has Notes

A meditation on nostalgia, inner-child healing, and the summer invitation to slow down and listen to the parts of ourselves we've left behind. Published for the &Rise newsletter readership.

"She's still here, and she's ready to thrive."

Library Blog · South College 2024

A Climate of Change

An outreach piece written for the South College library blog connecting students to sustainability resources, actionable habits, and NASA climate data. Pairs with a curated Climate Change Subject Guide.

"The opportunity to make a difference starts in our communities and on our campuses."

A philosophical essay on the courage required to speak your truth — the internal adversary, the risk of connection, and the irreducible value of honesty in relationships with others and oneself.

Teaching & Instruction

Learning environments
that work.

Instructional design isn't just about building courses — it's about anticipating where people get lost and building a bridge. Here's where I do that work.

●

Canvas LMS Course Development

South College, 2022–Present

Build and maintain Canvas course pages in collaboration with the instructional design team. Use HTML and CSS to format content, structure courseware, and create accessible, navigable learning environments for campus and online students.

●

Instructional Guides & Documentation

South College, 2022–Present

Develop and maintain technical guides and user procedures that support consistent system use across faculty, staff, and students. Translate complex technical processes into plain-language documentation that empowers self-sufficiency.

●

One-on-One Technology Support

South College Resource Center, 2022–Present

Provide scheduled and walk-in tech appointments for students navigating LMS access, courseware issues, and digital tools. Act as a connector between learners and the systems they need — translating technical barriers into actionable next steps.

●

Community Technology Literacy

Brevard County Public Library, 2017–2020

Helped patrons navigate technology access, account troubleshooting, and digital resource questions in a high-volume public library setting, making technology accessible to diverse community members.

Content Needed

Your narrative bio — 300 to 400 words

Write in first person, in your own voice. Open with a specific moment or image that captures why folklore, mythology, and hidden histories pull at you — not a statement of purpose, but a memory or scene. Then move outward: the graduate work, the library, the novel-in-progress, where you're going next.

Things to weave in:

  • Why the old stories specifically — what it is about narratives that survived centuries that you find so alive
  • Your Iron Age Israel novel — what drew you to that world, what question you're trying to answer through it
  • The connection between being a librarian and being a writer (you spend your days helping people find what they're looking for — in research, in systems, in the stacks — and your writing does the same thing in a different register)
  • Vietnam and what the location-independent future looks like to you
  • One sentence that captures your particular way of seeing

Voice: warm, specific, a little literary. Let the details do the work. No bullet points, no credentials list, no "I am passionate about."

Services

What we can
build together.

Every project I take on starts from the same place: a story that isn't quite landing yet, and a thread somewhere inside it that, once found, changes everything.

01

Developmental Editing

For fiction & literary nonfiction authors

Big-picture manuscript feedback focused on the architecture of your story: structure, pacing, character arc, thematic coherence, and narrative voice. I read the whole thing, sit with it, and write you an editorial letter that tells you exactly what's working, what isn't, and why — and what to do about it.

What's included

  • Full manuscript read
  • Detailed editorial letter
  • In-line manuscript notes
  • Two-hour follow-up call

Not this: line editing, copyediting, or proofreading. This is structural work.

02

Narrative Strategy Consulting

For filmmakers, podcasters & production companies

Story structure consultation for projects that have momentum but need architecture. Whether you're developing a feature, building a podcast series, or untangling a documentary that's lost its spine — I help you find the shape of the thing and make it hold together from beginning to end.

What's included

  • Story structure sessions (remote)
  • Arc and character development
  • Series or episode planning
  • Written notes after each session

Not this: screenwriting, scriptwriting, or production work.

03

AI Content Strategy

For cultural institutions, publishers & archives

Organizations integrating AI into editorial and research workflows need someone who understands both the technology and what gets lost when narrative quality and cultural accuracy slip through the cracks. I help you build AI workflows that serve your mission instead of diluting it.

What's included

  • AI editorial workflow design
  • Narrative quality review
  • Cultural accuracy consultation
  • Team training and documentation

Not this: ghostwriting or AI-generated content production.
